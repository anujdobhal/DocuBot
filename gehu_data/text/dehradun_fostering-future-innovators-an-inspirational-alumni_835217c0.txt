The School of Agriculture at Graphic Era Hill University recently facilitated a transformative interaction session with Mr. Abhishek Singh, an esteemed alumnus from the 2018-2022 batch and currently serving as Assistant Manager at Patanjali Organic Research Institute Pvt. Ltd. During this engaging session, Mr. Singh shared his inspiring journey, highlighting innovative approaches to sustainable farming and the significance of organic fertilizers in modern agriculture.
His presentation captivated students, instigating thought-provoking discussions on the future of agriculture and the role of sustainability in addressing global challenges. Mr. Singh's firsthand insights not only enriched the students’ academic experiences but also motivated them to consider careers in sustainable agricultural practices.
This event underscores Graphic Era Hill University’s commitment to bridging the gap between academic learning and industry expertise, fostering a new generation of agricultural leaders equipped to drive change in the sector.
