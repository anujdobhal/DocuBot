If you’re passionate about engineering, innovation, and cutting-edge technology, the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) at Graphic Era Hill University is the ideal place to begin your journey.
Our department is committed to excellence in education, research, and innovation in the field of Electronics and Communication Engineering. We aim to nurture industry-ready engineers who can address real-world challenges with confidence and creativity—both in India and across the globe.
To support this vision, the department offers state-of-the-art infrastructure, including advanced laboratories such as the INTEL Lab, Cadence Lab, Wireless Sensor Network Lab, Texas Instrumentation Lab, and the Polymer Sensors & Actuators Lab. These labs are designed to complement the curriculum and provide students with a strong foundation in practical and research-oriented learning. In addition to physical infrastructure, students also benefit from access to premium industry-standard software tools such as MathWorks Suite, Xilinx, Genesys, OrCAD Circuit Design & PCB Tools, and the Cadence University Bundle. This combination of hands-on experience and technological exposure empowers students to develop skills that are directly applicable in professional environments.
Our department also emphasizes research, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration, encouraging students to participate in projects, internships, and technical events that build critical thinking and problem-solving capabilities. Faculty members play an active role in mentoring students for national and international competitions, publishing research, and pursuing innovations with real-world impact. We also maintain strong industry linkages to ensure seamless transition from academics to careers through guest lectures, industrial visits, and placement training. Over the years, our alumni have built successful careers in top organizations across sectors like electronics, telecommunications, embedded systems, VLSI design, and IoT, both in India and globally. Their success stands as a testament to the quality education and holistic development provided by the department, making it a distinguished choice for anyone aspiring to excel in the field of Electronics and Communication Engineering.
