If you are passionate about Mechanical Engineering and want to be part of an inventive and progressive community, the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Graphic Era Hill University cordially invites you.
The Department of Mechanical Engineering at Graphic Era Hill University warmly welcomes you. The department has a long-standing history of providing exceptional educational and research opportunities to its students. Many of its alumni have become successful mechanical engineers, researchers, and leaders in various national and international organizations.
The department boasts well-qualified faculty members and industry experts who have received education from renowned universities across the world. They have achievements in diverse fields, including robotics, manufacturing engineering, thermal engineering, materials science, and more. Additionally, they have received several national and international awards and serve on the editorial boards/reviewers of numerous esteemed journals in the field.
The department offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Mechanical Engineering that aim to provide students with practical knowledge. Students can specialize in their areas of interest by choosing from a wide range of electives. The curriculum covers topics like Manufacturing Technology, Robotics, Thermal Engineering, Materials Science, CAD/CAM, Mechatronics, and Fluid Mechanics. The courses include laboratory experience, workshops, conferences, and guest lectures, all designed with a practical approach in mind.
The department encourages students to participate in various activities, competitions, conferences, and workshops, and engage in research projects. It has partnered with reputable organizations in the field and organizes site visits to provide students with the best possible training. The department provides state-of-the-art facilities, including labs equipped with specialized software such as ANSYS, MATLAB, and SolidWorks, widely used in mechanical engineering. The library also features various books, journals, and research papers for students to access and utilize.
The department takes pride in the achievements of its alumni and believes that their network is a valuable asset for current students. To leverage this network, the department frequently organizes alumni talks where the accomplished alumni share their experiences and provide career guidance to the students.
In addition to academic and research excellence, the Department emphasizes the importance of soft skills development in its students. The department provides opportunities for students to enhance their communication, leadership, and teamwork skills through various extracurricular activities and events. Students can participate in clubs and societies like the Robotics Club, the Society of Automotive Engineers, and the Entrepreneurship Club, where they can interact with like-minded peers and develop their skills beyond the classroom. The department arranges guest lectures and workshops by industry experts to help students gain insight into the practical aspects of the field and equip them with the skills necessary for a successful career.
