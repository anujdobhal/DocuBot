Teaching Pedagogies
The Department of management studies adopts innovative, student-centric teaching pedagogies designed to enhance conceptual understanding, practical proficiency, and industry readiness. Our approach integrates modern educational practices with emerging technologies to create an engaging, outcome-driven learning environment. Key pedagogical practices include:
Outcome-Based Education (OBE) All courses are structured around clearly defined learning outcomes to ensure measurable academic and professional growth.
Experiential & Hands-On Learning Strong emphasis is placed on practical learning through workshops, simulations, live projects, case analysis, analytics labs, and tool-based learning experiences.
Project-Based & Problem-Based Learning Students work on industry-relevant problems, case studies, mini-projects, business simulations, and capstone projects to develop analytical thinking and solution-oriented skills.
Flipped Classroom & Blended Learning Classroom time is enhanced through pre-class digital content, interactive discussions, collaborative exercises and applied learning activities.
Industry-Academia Engagement Regular expert lectures, corporate interactions, masterclasses, internships, certifications, and industry collaborations provide valuable real-world exposure.
Use of Modern ICT Tools Smart classrooms, LMS platforms, physical and virtual labs, AI development labs, data visualisation platforms, business simulation tools, and industry-standard coding environments support interactive and technology-driven learning.
Research-Oriented Teaching Students are encouraged to explore emerging trends, publish research papers, participate in hackathons, and engage in innovation-driven initiatives.
Continuous Assessment & Feedback Frequent quizzes, assignments, presentations, reviews, peer evaluations, and faculty mentoring help monitor progress and encourage continuous improvement.
Skill Development & Holistic Learning Equal emphasis is placed on communication skills, teamwork, leadership, professional ethics, adaptability, and lifelong learning to prepare students for dynamic global careers.

PEOs (Program Educational Objectives)
PEO1: To produce graduates having knowledge, ability and skill to apply basic principles of management to plan, execute, monitor and evaluate business operations. PEO2: To develop analytical aptitude and techno-managerial skills among students for effective coordination and communication for managing business organisations. PEO3: To inculcate leadership qualities, interpersonal skills and professional traits among the students to work individually and in team. PEO4: To sensitise the students towards issues like personal & professional ethics, environment conservation, culture and socio-political settings of immediate surroundings.
POs (Program Outcomes)
PO1: Apply knowledge of management theories and practices to solve business problems. PO2: Foster Analytical, critical thinking and techno-managerial abilities for data-based decision making. PO3: Ability to develop Value based Leadership ability. PO 4: Ability to understand, analyse and communicate global, economic, legal, and ethical aspects. PO 5: Ability to lead themselves and others in the achievement of organisational goals, contributing effectively to a team environment. PO 6: Ability to develop entrepreneurial skills using innovative practices.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the duration of the M.B.A Business Analytics program?
The programme is of 2 years (6 trimesters) duration, designed in accordance with applicable UGC/AICTE norms. The trimester system enables focused learning, continuous assessment, and wider course coverage.
What are the eligibility criteria for admission to M.B.A Business Analytics?
Candidates must have completed graduation from a recognised university/institution. Admission is based on merit, qualifying entrance test scores as applicable, and personal interview conducted by the University.
What are the career opportunities after completing M.B.A Business Analytics?
After completing an M.B.A., graduates can explore a wide range of career opportunities across industries. Popular roles include Business Analyst, Consultant, Marketing Manager, Finance Executive, HR Manager, Operations Manager, Strategy Associate, and Entrepreneurial ventures. Banking, fintech, and insurance offer strong financial and analytical career opportunities. Graduates may also build careers in marketing, branding, retail management, aviation, hospital administration, healthcare, sports management, and other specialised sectors. With growing industry demand for digital and data-led decision making, graduates may also pursue emerging opportunities in analytics, digital products, fintech, digital marketing, and technology-enabled management roles.
What is the syllabus of M.B.A Business Analytics?
The MBA curriculum covers key areas of management such as Applied Programming, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, LLM, Business Analytics along with Strategy, Economics, Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Operations, Entrepreneurship, and Business Environment. Specialisations offer students the opportunity to learn niche sectors such as aviation, hospital administration, retail and sports management, and other latest industry-relevant disciplines. In addition, the MBA programme offers contemporary exposure to research projects, internships, certifications, and capstone learning experiences aligned with current industry needs.
Why choose Graphic Era for M.B.A Business Analytics?
Graphic Era offers a strong industry-oriented curriculum, academic environment, experienced faculty, modern infrastructure, innovation ecosystem, and growing focus on emerging areas such as Analytics, AI, and Digital Transformation. Students benefit from practical exposure through projects, internships, certifications, and career development support.
