The School of Commerce at Graphic Era Hill University, Dehradun is renowned for its commitment to providing high-quality education, practical training, and industry exposure to its students. The school has garnered a reputation for producing graduates who are well-prepared to take on the challenges of the modern business world.
These programs have been designed to equip graduates with the knowledge and technical skills necessary to understand and participate in the modern business world, preparing them for subsequent graduate studies and achieving the highest level of success in their professional careers.
The school offers a range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in commerce. The programs are designed to provide students with a strong foundation in theory, practical knowledge, and industry exposure.
To further enhance the learning experience of students, the School of Commerce has state-of-the-art infrastructure and facilities. The school has a well-stocked library that provides students with access to a vast collection of books, journals, and research papers. The library also subscribes to various online databases, providing students with the latest research in their respective fields. The school also has a computer lab that is equipped with modern equipment and software that allows students to gain practical knowledge of various accounting and management tools.
The school is committed to providing students with industry exposure and practical training, enabling them to develop the skills and knowledge required to succeed in their respective fields. The school has tie-ups with leading companies and organizations, and students are encouraged to participate in internships and training programs.
The faculty members of the school are highly qualified and experienced, with many of them holding doctoral degrees in their respective fields. The faculty members bring a wealth of industry experience to the classroom and use modern teaching techniques such as case studies and simulations to ensure that students gain hands-on experience in their respective fields.
The school also has an active research program that encourages faculty members and students to undertake research projects in their respective fields. The school provides students with opportunities to present their research work at national and international conferences, enabling them to gain exposure and recognition in the academic community.
The school of Commerce at Graphic Era Hill University has a strong focus on innovation and entrepreneurship. The school encourages students to develop their innovative ideas and supports them in turning those ideas into successful businesses. Many students have gone on to start their own businesses, thanks to the school’s focus on innovation and entrepreneurship.
The school has a strong focus on sustainable development, which is reflected in the curriculum and research activities. The school aims to produce graduates who are socially responsible and environmentally conscious.
Graduates of the school of Commerce at Graphic Era Hill University, have a wide range of career opportunities available to them. They can become an accountant, financial analyst, marketing manager, business consultant, or even an entrepreneur. In addition to these career options, graduates can pursue further studies and research careers in commerce and management.
In conclusion, the School of Commerce at GEHU is dedicated to providing high-quality education, practical training, and industry exposure to equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary to thrive in the world of commerce and management. The school’s focus on holistic development ensures that students are well-prepared to face the challenges of the modern world. The school’s commitment to producing socially responsible graduates makes it a desirable destination for students who seek not only academic excellence but also a strong sense of social responsibility.
