The School of Management at Graphic Era Hill University is a premier academic department that offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in various fields of management. The department is committed to providing quality education and training to students in the field of management and preparing them to become future leaders and entrepreneurs.
The School of Management has a team of highly qualified and experienced faculty members who are dedicated to providing the best possible education to the students. The faculty members have diverse academic and industry experience and are actively involved in research and consulting activities. The faculty members use innovative teaching methodologies that include case studies, and group discussions to enhance the learning experience of the students.
The undergraduate program in management provides students with a strong foundation in various areas of management, including marketing, finance, human resource management, and operations management. The program also includes courses on business ethics, communication skills, and entrepreneurship.
The postgraduate program in management provides students with in-depth knowledge and practical skills in various areas of management. The program includes courses on advanced topics such as strategic management, organizational behaviour, and international business. The department also offers specialized programs in areas such as marketing, finance, human resource management, and operations management.
The department has a strong industry interface that encourages students to participate in internships and projects with leading organizations. The department also organizes various seminars, workshops, and guest lectures by industry experts to provide students with an understanding of the latest developments in the field of management.
The department also has an active research program that inspires faculty members and students to conduct research in their respective fields. The department provides students with opportunities to present their research work at national and international conferences, enabling them to gain exposure and recognition in the academic community.
The department has a strong placement record, and its graduates are highly sought after by top companies in the technology industry. The department has a dedicated placement cell that assists students in finding job opportunities and provides career counselling services. Apart from this, the department has a strong alumni network that includes successful entrepreneurs, executives, and managers who are actively involved in the growth and development of the department. The alumni network provides students with mentorship and networking opportunities, which are critical for their career growth.
Completing a degree in management from Graphic Era Hill University opens up a world of career opportunities for students. With their skills and knowledge, graduates can pursue successful careers, higher studies, and research opportunities in this growing field.
