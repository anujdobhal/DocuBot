University Trips
The Campus might be the best place to be, but you would still need a break at times. The University takes care of that too, with Trekking and Hiking Trips organized every semester at a massive scale, with even the Top Brass participating with the students.
The University’s close proximity to Trekking Hot Trails such as Jharipani and Bhimtal, offers a great chance to experience the breathtaking view of the Middle Himalayas with cool wind in your hair and steep climbs to tackle. There is a trekking trip organized every semester to the scenic hill station of Mussorie, with the President of the University leading the trek.
There are major treks available via student clubs for the extreme trekking enthusiasts such as the Pindari Glacier. You’re now in the heart of the Himalayas, the ride is indeed going to be steep and splendid
