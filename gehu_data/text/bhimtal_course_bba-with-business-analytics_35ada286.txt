Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
PEO 1: Foundation in Business Management and Analytical Decision-Making:
The graduate of BBA with business analytics program will equipped on building a strong foundation in core business disciplines along with analytical reasoning skills. Students learn to interpret data, evaluate alternatives, and make informed decisions. The program integrates theoretical knowledge with practical applications, enabling learners to solve complex business problems effectively in dynamic environments.
PEO 2: Application of Modern Analytics and AI Tools
Program graduate will be prepared on how to use modern analytics, data visualization, and AI-enabled tools in business contexts. It emphasizes hands-on learning and practical exposure to emerging technologies. Graduate develop the ability to transform data into actionable insights, helping organizations improve efficiency, innovation, and decision-making processes.
PEO 3: Communication, Consulting, and Teamwork Skills
The PEO aims to enhance communication, teamwork, and consulting capabilities among Graduate. It develops their ability to present ideas clearly, collaborate in diverse teams, and engage in problem-solving. Through projects and presentations, students gain essential professional skills required for corporate and entrepreneurial success.
PEO 4: Ethical and Responsible Professional Development
Graduate will be nurtured on ethically responsible professional’s behaviors who will demonstrate integrity and accountability in business practices. Graduate are trained to consider social and environmental impacts while making decisions. The program promotes responsible behavior across business functions, preparing graduates to handle ethical challenges and contribute positively to organizations and society.
PEO 5: Career Growth and Higher Education Pathways
This objective focuses on preparing students for higher education, professional development, and analytics-driven careers. It equips them with relevant skills, industry exposure, and adaptability. Graduates are encouraged to pursue advanced studies and certifications while being capable of building successful careers in a competitive global business environment.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is a BBA in Business Analytics?
A BBA in Business Analytics is an undergraduate program that combines business management education with data analysis, statistics, and technology. It prepares students to interpret data, generate insights, and support decision-making in various business domains.
What are the key subjects covered in this program?
The program typically includes subjects like business management, statistics, data analytics, financial management, marketing, operations, data visualization, machine learning basics, and use of analytical tools such as Excel, Python, and BI software.
What skills will I gain from this program?
Students develop analytical thinking, problem-solving, data interpretation, communication, teamwork, and technical skills. They also learn to use modern tools and technologies for analyzing and visualizing business data effectively.
What are the career opportunities after completing this program?
Graduates can work as business analysts, data analysts, marketing analysts, financial analysts, or consultants. Opportunities exist across industries such as banking, consulting, IT, e-commerce, and healthcare.
Is coding required in this program?
No, the program starts from foundational levels, so prior coding experience is not mandatory.
What is the eligibility criteria for admission?
Students typically need to have completed 10+2 (or equivalent) from a recognized board. Some institutions may require minimum percentage criteria or entrance exams.
How is this program different from a regular BBA?
Unlike a traditional BBA, this program focuses more on data-driven decision-making, analytics tools, and technology integration, making graduates more suitable for modern, data-centric business roles.
Are internships part of the program?
Yes, most programs include internships, live projects, or industry training. These provide practical exposure and help students apply their knowledge in real-world business scenarios.
What higher education options are available after this program?
Graduates can pursue MBA (Business Analytics, Finance, Marketing), MSc in Analytics, Data Science programs, or professional certifications like CFA, FRM, or specialized analytics courses.
Is Business Analytics a good career choice?
Yes, business analytics is a rapidly growing field due to increasing reliance on data for decision-making. It offers strong career prospects, competitive salaries, and opportunities across diverse industries globally.
