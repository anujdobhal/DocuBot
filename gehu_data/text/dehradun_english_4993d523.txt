The Department of English at Graphic Era Hill University is an interdisciplinary department that offers courses and programs in various fields of study related to human behavior, culture, and society. The department emphasizes the study of human experiences, values, and behaviors, and how they shape society as a whole.
Ever since its inception in 2014, the Department of English at Graphic Era Hill University (GEHU) has been committed to fostering an environment of true learning. Our mission is to transform each student into not only a successful professional but also a responsible global citizen. Over the past few years, our department has experienced significant growth, with student enrollment soaring from an initial 14 to around 200 in just few years. This expansion is a testament to our quality of education and the value we provide to our students.
Academic Excellence and Research Orientation
Our curriculum for undergraduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D. courses is meticulously designed in accordance with the guidelines of the University Grants Commission and the Choice Based Credit System .We place a strong emphasis on research orientation, fostering literary creativity, developing analytical skills, and enhancing employability. Our Board of Studies is chaired by distinguished professors from prestigious institutions such as Jawaharlal Nehru University, Indian Institutes of Technology, and various Central Universities. This ensures that our syllabi are contemporary, comprehensive, and challenging.
Innovative Teaching Pedagogies
In keeping with the latest trends in education, our teaching methodologies are aligned with Practical Based Learning approaches. This hands-on learning experience is crucial in preparing our students for real-world challenges. Our classroom activities are enriched with a variety of extra-curricular literary events, including Open Mic sessions, skit Competition, poetry readings, and group discussions. Theatrical performances, guest lectures, and workshops. These activities not only enhance the learning experience but also help in nurturing a vibrant literary community
Exposure to Global Literary Events
We believe in providing our students with exposure beyond the classroom. To this end, we organize visits to prominent international Literature festivals such as the Valley of Words. These experiences are invaluable in broadening our students’ horizons and deepening their appreciation of global literary trends.
Career Development and Placement Support
The Department of English at GEHU is dedicated to the career development of our students. We consistently encourage participation in multiple placement drives throughout the year, facilitating opportunities with reputed publishing firms and other organizations. Additionally, we offer access to Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) as part of our self-learning practices, further equipping our students with the skills and knowledge necessary for their professional growth.
Faculty and Student Excellence
Our department is renowned for its dedicated, disciplined and forward-thinking faculty, who are passionate about their teaching and research. They play a crucial role in maintaining the high standards of education that our department is known for. Equally, our students are a source of pride, contributing to the department’s success with their enthusiasm, creativity, and commitment to learning.
